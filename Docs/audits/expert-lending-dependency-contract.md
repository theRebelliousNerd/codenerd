# Expert lending must preserve usable dependency graphs

**Decision:** make persona-independent expertise an explicit, dependency-valid export. Keep foreign identity out, keep missing-dependency enforcement fail-closed, and leave optional guidance subject to relevance, policy and budget.

**Evidence status:** static inspection of public CodeNERD main at `8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20`, revalidated after main advanced during this review, with source heads checked on 2026-10-29 at 21:48 UTC. This is a source-predicted composition gap, not an observed incident. No implementation, tests, inference, training, profiling or provider calls were run.

## The producer and export contracts do not compose

### Generated bodies retain identity dependencies

The [generator](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/init/agents.go#L283-L305) creates mandatory `x/identity`, optional methodology requiring that identity, and optional domain guidance requiring identity plus methodology when methodology exists. Empty bodies produce no atom. [Atomization](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/init/agents_atomize.go#L253-L268) propagates dependencies to every piece.

Production creation [runs admission before writing YAML](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/init/agents.go#L134-L141). Admission can reject bodies, but always retains the base identity; its dependency cleanup therefore retains surviving bodies' identity edges ([admission and cleanup](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/init/agents_admission.go#L89-L149)). The witness requires nonempty, retained bodies, not every generated agent. This is also the [user-agent definition writer's generator](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/agent_definition.go#L36-L95), not merely hypothetical handcrafted data.

### Storage preserves the edges

The [synchronizer](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/sync/synchronizer.go#L117-L165) parses each agent's YAML and replaces atoms in that agent's knowledge database. Dependencies are [context tags](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/atoms.go#L379-L407), [persisted by the loader](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/loader.go#L500-L507) and [hydrated into DependsOn](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_db.go#L130-L131). No schema-loss premise is needed.

Boot [repairs and synchronizes definitions](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/factory.go#L1756-L1766) and [registers discovered agent databases and the expert roster](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/factory.go#L1937-L1951). The [project corpus is separately initialized](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/factory.go#L1799-L1822). The conditional case excludes an independently collected same-ID identity from another source; per-agent synchronization is not itself such a rescue.

### Ordinary sessions can reach the exporter

The session helper [calls experts.Select](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/expert_context.go#L19-L37); the executor [selects once, builds context from its agent names and invokes JIT](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/executor.go#L1370-L1404). Context construction [assigns cc.Experts](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/executor.go#L1570-L1574) while [normal verb routing selects the speaking persona](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/executor.go#L1616-L1620). A healthy ordinary coder turn can therefore request expert `x` without becoming `x`.

The [lending contract](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_experts.go#L13-L28) promises knowledge without a second voice. [Source resolution](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_experts.go#L49-L72) skips the current speaker, duplicates and missing databases. However, [lendable and collection](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_experts.go#L81-L102) exclude only identity-category atoms and return the original nonidentity pointers. Their dependency edges remain unchanged. [Source assembly](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_sources.go#L69-L100) adds these after embedded, project and own-speaker sources.

### A tentative dependency name cannot replace its object

Do not claim Mangle necessarily rejects the body immediately. Its [recursive tentative rule](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/core/defaults/jit_compiler.mg#L353-L360) can derive a prerequisite ID without a corresponding candidate object. But [skeleton materialization](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/selector.go#L1045-L1067) and [flesh materialization](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/selector.go#L1252-L1274) map results back to original candidates; an absent identity has no renderable object. [Section expansion](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/sections.go#L38-L49) also indexes candidates, rather than fetching missing identities.

The compiler [constructs the default resolver](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler.go#L341-L349), whose [default is allowMissingDeps false](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/resolver.go#L35-L39). It [transitively prunes absent prerequisites](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/resolver.go#L74-L114). The [pipeline](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_pipeline.go#L301-L336) runs selection, section expansion, resolution, then fitting. Thus a generated body cannot survive resolution without its identity object, even if otherwise selected. This is the resolver correctly refusing an incompatible export graph, not a reason to weaken it.

## Current consultation routes narrow the witness

Current main also [adds consultations after JIT and before the model loop](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/executor.go#L1448-L1462). This matters: [language evidence has confidence 40 and import evidence 90](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/core/defaults/policy/shard_experts.mg#L78-L85); [execution requires above 80 and advice above 40](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/core/defaults/policy/shards.mg#L224-L236), with [those decisions deriving expert_spawn](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/core/defaults/policy/shard_experts.mg#L110-L116).

Consequently a language-only match can name an expert for JIT without spawning it. With no spawn decisions, [withExpertConsultations returns the existing prompt](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/expert_spawn.go#L63-L72). Import-strength evidence can instead trigger a direct-expert consultation and [append its answer](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/session/expert_spawn.go#L82-L105). That separate reading may compensate for lost pushed bodies and adds costs of its own. It is not proof that the lending graph is valid.

The strongest witness therefore uses language-only evidence, no matching imports or policy overrides, and no separate expert consultation. Do not generalize it to every import-driven turn or to campaign routes that may advise or assign an expert speaker.

## A precise conditional witness

Use generated and admitted `x/identity`, `x/methodology` and `x/domain`. Put a unique useful marker in an optional body, absent from knowledge rows and other sources. Assume a healthy matching roster, registered database, successful expert derivation, noncancelled compile and no independently collected `x/identity`.

For example, use an ordinary coder `/fix` turn on `policy.mg`, a healthy `/mangle` language classification and a ready matching expert, but only language evidence. Confirm confidence 40 and no spawn decision. Then observe:

1. `x` survives into `cc.Experts`.
2. Collection supplies its bodies but not its identity.
3. Even granting semantic and Mangle admission of the body, no identity object materializes.
4. Resolution removes the body before budgeting.

“Granting admission” isolates the dependency failure; it does not imply every optional body deserves inclusion. Boot [installs a vector searcher when an embedding engine exists](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/factory.go#L1793-L1796); the [production searcher](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/vector_searcher.go#L55-L138) scans registered databases with model and dimension checks. An eligible vector, relevance approval and sufficient budget remain separate conditions for a positive repaired case.

## What this does not establish

A direct expert-speaker compile [loads the full shard graph](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_sources.go#L85-L95), while pushed-source resolution avoids duplicating that speaker. This is a strong control, not evidence that all its optional bodies always render.

Knowledge rows take a separate [bounded lexical path](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_experts.go#L108-L135) that excludes agent identity and mission rows. [Pushed conversion](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_db.go#L371-L383) supplies no foreign shard tag, and the [constructor](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_db.go#L451-L468) adds no `DependsOn`. Those rows may still deliver expertise. Custom independent bodies may work too; other sources and tools may compensate. The evidence does not establish total knowledge disappearance, guaranteed answer-quality loss, tool-authority bypass or a cache-miss defect.

The existing [real boot integration fixture](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/shard_expert_boot_test.go#L20-L42) uses mandatory methodology without an identity dependency. Its [assertions](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/system/shard_expert_boot_test.go#L140-L149) check methodology and knowledge presence while excluding foreign identity and self-description. Keep this valuable positive control; it does not compose the actual generated optional dependency graph. Tests were read, not run.

## Repair the export boundary without weakening execution policy

Prefer producer-authored, persona-independent lendable graphs: separate reusable substantive prerequisites from voice identity, then let both speaking and lending views reference appropriate shared guidance. Preserve authored content and direct-speaker behavior.

An alternative is an explicit, validated export projection. Remove an identity-only edge only after establishing that the dependent content is genuinely persona-independent. Preserve substantive prerequisites, sections, context constraints, tool requirements, conflicts and pins. If content genuinely requires the expert persona, mark it non-lendable with a reason rather than silently pretending independence.

Projection must create copies, including dependency slices that might otherwise alias shared state. [Catalog atoms are shared immutable values](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/prompt/compiler_generation.go#L195-L202); mutating their dependency slices in place could contaminate later direct-speaker compiles. Bind projected graphs to source generations and a projection identity or version, and include both in any reuse contract. Existing expert-aware source vectors and context hashing are not missing-key findings.

Do not globally permit missing dependencies, erase arbitrary edges, restore foreign identity, force all expert bodies mandatory, or copy the whole knowledge base into the prompt. Mangle remains the executive selector; relevance vetoes, budgets and independent tool authority remain binding.

Existing [curated definitions are preserved](https://github.com/theRebelliousNerd/codenerd/blob/8decae5763cb88bbd842795ca81d3e2559ec2b20/internal/init/agents.go#L69-L83), so changing only new generation leaves older definitions untouched. Make migration versioned, opt-in and reviewable, without overwriting curation. Changed text or IDs may require re-embedding and migration work; that is a cost to plan, not authorization for network spending.

## Proposed evaluations and costs

All checks below are unrun. Build the primary fixture through the real generator and admission path, sync it into a registered database, and exercise the production session's normal coder route. Use deterministic eligible embeddings and a fake embedding engine with the production vector backend and kernel; bypassing the selector would miss the composition boundary.

Trace the unique marker through candidates, selection, resolution, fitting and the actual model-request construction. Require its presence only in relevant, permitted, sufficiently budgeted repaired cases; always require foreign identity absence. Compare language-only confidence 40 with import confidence 90 and successful, failed or absent consultation answers. Keep the JIT-body result distinct from any consultation compensation. Compare the existing independent-method fixture with generated graphs, direct speaking with pushed lending, and positive cases with empty `cc.Experts`, absent database, admission-rejected body, ineligible vector and Mangle relevance refusal.

Also cover tool, provider, shard and conflict vetoes; tight budgets; explicit duplicate identity sources; multi-section graphs; missing substantive prerequisites; cancellation and source refresh. Mixed direct/pushed compilations must leave shared catalogs unchanged. A passing collector-only test is insufficient, and preserving post-budget dependency closure remains the earlier audit's obligation.

Graph classification, projection and validation can be linear in vertices plus edges per source generation. Correctness comes first; generation-bound immutable projections can then avoid rebuilding the graph each compile. Account for extra allocations, retained memory, database reads, migration review and possible re-embedding. More useful guidance can consume tokens and displace other atoms.

Measure cold/warm compilation latency, CPU, allocations, retained memory, database work, embedding and model requests, total prompt tokens, and task retries or acceptance on matched workloads. Include negative controls and direct-speaker regressions, not just marker recovery. No savings or quality improvement has been measured; the immediate objective is a usable export that preserves declared dependencies and authority.
